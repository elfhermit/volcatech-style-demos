Key capabilities

What the platform brings, built in.

SIEM with SOAR included

Playbooks gather evidence and drive response automatically; case management and SOC KPI dashboards are part of the platform, not an add-on. Where the next step is a judgement call rather than a routine one, the playbook pauses for a person to make it.

Threat intelligence and UEBA

Detections are enriched with VirusTotal, Google Cloud Threat Intelligence and Mandiant, while behavioural analytics flags unusual sign-ins, brute force and abnormal traffic.

Gemini-assisted investigation

Ask questions in natural language, get case summaries, and draft detection rules and playbooks — threat hunting without learning another query language first.

Fast search, long retention

Searches run at speed with no hot-cold migrations, logs are retained for one year by default, and 300+ integrations connect endpoint, network, ITSM and ticketing tools.

Common questions

What evaluators ask.

01If we run this, do we still need web-layer detection (WDR) and EDR products?

Yes. The platform correlates, investigates and automates on the telemetry it receives; something still has to produce that telemetry at the endpoint and at the web layer. CyberEyes and an EDR agent are those sources, and Google Security Operations is where their findings meet the rest of your logs.

02How much of the onboarding is parser work?

Less than with an on-premises SIEM. Parsing for common sources — on-premises systems, the major public clouds, endpoint agents and Google Cloud's own protections — comes with the platform, so most sources are a mapping and validation exercise rather than a development one. In-house applications with unusual log formats still take work. We scope that against your actual source list before committing to a timeline.

03Can we keep logs beyond the platform's default retention?

Yes. Logs can be exported to cloud storage and held for as long as your own policy requires, which is the usual route for obligations that outlast the default window in the platform.

04Does the platform remove the need for analysts?

No. Playbooks collect evidence and carry out routine steps, and Gemini shortens the search work, but decisions about containment and communication stay with people. Where there is nobody to staff the console, our SOC can operate it for you.

05How is Google Security Operations charged?

Charges follow the volume of data you ingest rather than the number of users or nodes, so how you design log collection matters more than headcount does. Contact us and we will model it against the sources you actually need.

06What does UEBA actually catch?

Patterns that are hard to write as fixed rules: a sign-in from an unfamiliar location, device or hour; repeated failures that add up to a brute-force attempt; a single account suddenly raising alerts across many systems; traffic well out of line with an entity's history. Each surfaces as a case alongside every other detection, for an analyst to confirm rather than to dig for.

07How far does Gemini go in an investigation?

It shortens the reading and the writing: it summarises a case, turns a plain-language question into a search, drafts detection rules and playbooks, and assembles what is known about a threat. It does not decide. Containment, escalation and communication stay with your analysts, and a drafted rule or playbook only takes effect once someone puts it into service.

08Does this only make sense if we are already on Google Cloud?

No. The platform sits above the systems it watches rather than inside them, so logs from an estate that runs on-premises, in another provider's cloud, or across both are collected the same way. Adopting it does not require moving a workload anywhere. What running Google Cloud adds is that its own security logs arrive natively.

09Can we feed our own threat intelligence into it?

Yes. Alongside the sources that come with the platform — VirusTotal, Google Cloud Threat Intelligence and Mandiant — feeds you subscribe to or produce yourself can be imported through the API and used to enrich detections and cases in the same way. Intelligence your team already curates keeps its value instead of being set aside when the platform changes.

10Will a playbook act on a production system by itself?

Only where the playbook has been built to. The work that runs unattended is the collection: pulling together the account and the device behind an alert, checking the indicators against the intelligence sources, and assembling the result into a case that can be read rather than reconstructed. Whether a step that changes the state of a system runs on its own or waits at a decision point is set when the playbook is written, so the boundary is yours to draw and to revisit.
