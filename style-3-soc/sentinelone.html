Common questions

What evaluators ask.

01How does SentinelOne compare with ThreatSonar?

Both cover endpoints, but they are built for different jobs. SentinelOne is oriented towards continuous prevention and automatic containment as part of daily operations; ThreatSonar opens with an agentless assessment and is built for investigation depth, drawing on TeamT5's research into Asia-Pacific intrusion groups. The right choice follows whether your priority is stopping attacks without an analyst present, or establishing what is already inside.

02What happens on a device that is off the corporate network?

Judgements are made on the endpoint itself rather than in a cloud queue, so files are assessed before they run even with no connection available. That matters for laptop fleets and for sites where connectivity is intermittent.

03Can its detections feed the SIEM we already run?

Yes. The platform exposes an API for integration, and Google Security Operations takes endpoint telemetry as one of its standard sources, so endpoint detections can sit alongside web and cloud logs in one console. Under our ESS programme the same feed also reaches the Volcatech SOC.

04How much work is a mixed Windows, macOS and Linux estate?

A single agent covers Windows, macOS, Linux and Kubernetes, so there is no separate product per platform to buy, tune and report on. Rollout is normally staged by asset group. We size the waves against your estate and agree the schedule before the first one.

05How is SentinelOne licensed?

Licensing depends on how many endpoints you protect and the capability set each group needs, so the shape of your estate decides the scope. Contact us and we will size it against your real fleet.

06Does the console have to run in the vendor's cloud?

No. The management console is available as SaaS but can also be deployed on premises, and the vendor supports hybrid and physically isolated environments. For organisations with data-residency or segregation constraints, the deployment model stays a choice rather than a limitation. Tell us where your data has to live and we will scope the fit.
