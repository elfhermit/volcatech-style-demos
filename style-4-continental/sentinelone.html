SentinelOne Singularity

AI-powered endpoint protection that detects and stops attacks at machine speed — and restores affected endpoints when ransomware strikes.

1.0 Pain points

The problems this specification addresses.

  1. Alert fatigueAnalysts drown in noisy detections; triage takes hours, not minutes.
  2. Ransomware speedAttacks move faster than any manual response process can react.
  3. Endpoint blind spotsMixed Windows, macOS and Linux fleets leave gaps between tools.

2.0 Key capabilities

Functional description.

  1. Behavioral AI detectionMalicious behavior identified on-device — no dependence on signature updates.
  2. Autonomous responseKill, quarantine and isolate execute in real time, prior to analyst review.
  3. Ransomware rollbackEndpoints and encrypted files revert to pre-attack state on supported platforms.
  4. Full attack contextProcesses, files and connections correlated into a single readable storyline.

3.0 Business benefits

Expected outcomes after deployment.

  1. Minutes, not hoursMean time to respond drops sharply with autonomous containment.
  2. Leaner operationsAutomated triage frees analysts for investigation work.
  3. Faster recoveryRollback shortens downtime after ransomware attempts.
