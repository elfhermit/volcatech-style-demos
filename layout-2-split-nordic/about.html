About Volcatech

One team for cloud, security and operations.

Who we are

Volcatech was founded by engineers from international AIoT, security software and cloud service companies. We work with organisations across Europe and East Asia, with our engineering team based in Taiwan.

What we do

We design and run Google Cloud environments, deploy and tune EDR and SIEM platforms, and keep both under 24/7 monitoring — including cloud migration, hybrid architectures, data and AI engineering, and the security services we build ourselves.

Our aim is to be the turn-key partner that small and mid-sized organisations rely on for IT transformation, security transformation and AI adoption — one team, one contract, one point of accountability.
